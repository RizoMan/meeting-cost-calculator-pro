# Privacy Policy for Meeting Cost Pro

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-15

## 1. Introduction
Meeting Cost Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by Meeting Cost Pro.

## 2. Data Collection
**We do not collect any personal data.**
All meeting data, costs, and team information are stored **locally on your device** using SQLite. We do not have servers, and we do not transmit your meeting data to the cloud.

## 3. Local Storage
Your data is stored in a secure local database on your device. If you uninstall the application, this data will be permanently deleted unless you have backed up your device.

## 4. Subscription Information
Payments and subscriptions are processed by the respective app stores (Apple App Store or Google Play Store). We do not have access to your credit card information. We may receive anonymous receipts to validate your "Pro" status.
